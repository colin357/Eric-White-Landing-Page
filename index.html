Financing for the Home
You Actually Want

Whether you're building something traditional or thinking outside the box, we have the financing solutions to make it happen.

🏠

Modular Homes

Factory-built in sections and assembled on-site, modular homes qualify for conventional financing. We streamline the lending process from start to close.

🏡

Manufactured Homes

HUD-coded manufactured homes on permanent foundations can qualify for FHA, VA, and conventional loans. We know the requirements inside and out.

🏚

Barndominiums

Steel-frame barndominium financing is our specialty. We work with lenders who understand these unique properties and can structure loans that fit your build.

📦

Container Homes

Converted shipping container homes are gaining mainstream acceptance. We'll help you navigate the appraisal and lending process for your modern container build.

🏘

ADUs & Guest Houses

Accessory Dwelling Units — whether attached, detached, or above a garage — can be financed as part of your primary home loan or as a standalone investment.

🏗

Traditional Site-Built

New construction on your own lot or a builder's lot. We offer construction-to-permanent loans so you only go through the closing process once.
